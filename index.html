Background & Identity

From Grassroots Youth Leader to Professional Public Servant

Hon. Engr. Charlon Gonzales Caadlawon's public service began at the grassroots level as an elected Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) Member in Barangay Antipolo, Bagamanoc from 2009 to 2011. This early involvement gave him a clear understanding of the daily hardships and aspirations of rural youth and coastal families.

Over the subsequent 15 years, he dedicated himself to rigorous professional training in civil engineering and building systems. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Catanduanes State University (Batch of 2016), graduated from Bagamanoc RDHS with honors, and successfully passed both the Civil Engineering Board Examination in November 2016 and the Master Plumber Board Examination in February 2017.

“I chose public service because I have seen how government works at the national level, and this thought hit me: I can at least set a good example of an honest, competent public servant in our municipality.”
— Hon. Engr. Charlon G. Caadlawon

In the May 2025 Municipal Elections, the citizens of Bagamanoc elected him as the Number 1 Municipal Councilor with 4,321 votes. Today, he channels his technical credentials directly into evidence-based legislation and transparent governance.
