The Journey to Public Office

Councilor Charlon Gonzales Caadlawon began his leadership journey as an active Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) Member from 2009 to 2011 in Barangay Antipolo, Bagamanoc. Grounded in grassroots youth leadership, he witnessed firsthand the everyday challenges faced by barangay families and youth.

He then spent 15 years developing extensive technical experience as a licensed professional, graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Catanduanes State University (Batch of 2016). He successfully passed the Civil Engineering Licensure Examination in November 2016 and the Master Plumber Board Examination in February 2017.

“I chose public service because I have seen how the government works at the national level, and this thought hit me: I can at least set a good example to be an honest public servant in our municipality.”

— Hon. Charlon Gonzales Caadlawon

In the May 2025 Municipal Elections, he presented his platform of technical integrity and honest governance to the people of Bagamanoc. The electorate responded overwhelmingly, electing him as the Rank 1 Municipal Councilor with 4,321 votes.
